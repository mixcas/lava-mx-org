  //
  
  ***Y las entrañas gritaron…*** is a group show that delves into the alteration of Earth's natural and mineral compounds, a phenomenon exacerbated by the technological boom of the last century. The exhibition offers a critical reflection on the extractive practices of the 'Anthropobscence,' which perpetuate cycles of environmental violence with effects on both human and non-human life.
  
  The selection of works spans different temporal scales, from geological traces to the immediate impacts of landscape exploitation. By drilling into the Earth, sedimented layers of accumulated destruction emerge, an archive of consequences from human intervention. Through videos, sculptures, and actions, the show exposes this devastation and presents alternate narratives about the care of ecosystems and their vital dynamism.
